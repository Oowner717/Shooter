Field Guide to the Twenty and how everything moves

Twenty new objects for the field, and a gait for every object already on it. Thirty-six field bodies currently draw their approach from the same six routes, which is why they all read as one crowd walking slowly downhill.

The field, with eight gaits on it

One portal at the top, one machine at the bottom, and eight bodies in the space between. None of them is taking the route the game currently gives all of them. That is the whole proposal in one picture.

Two things the roster already says

Movement is drawn per BODY, not per TYPE

this.route = opts.route || weightedPick(ROUTES) sits in the Enemy constructor, so a 45-unit BULWARK and a 10-unit NEEDLE draw from the same six routes with the same weights. The routes differ only in how wide the approach bows and how fast the bow folds in. Two types in forty-three own any motion of their own: LURCHER, which pulses its accelerator, and DRIFT, which wanders. Everything else is the same walk at a different speed.

The palette already encodes eight families

Measured in CIELAB across all sixteen field types: every one lands within 31 dE of one of eight anchors, and the anchors are 30 to 142 dE apart. Nobody wrote that down, and it is the only room left in a palette where, per the working notes, every saturated hue is spoken for. So the twenty below take family hues rather than new ones: hue says what it does to you, shape says which one it is, gait says how it arrives.

The two anchors worth watching are STRANGE against HEAVY at 30 dE — violet and azure, the closest pair in the set — and INERT against SWARM at 38. A new body in either pair has to carry its distinction in silhouette, because the hue will not do it alone.

One rule constrains the harmless newcomers and is worth stating before it gets broken: check-build.mjs enforces grey means harmless in one direction only — the single grey #8fa9c4 may only sit on a harmless type, and every other type must carry at least 0.28 chroma. Harmless bodies may be coloured; SEED is violet. Five of the six drift-like objects below wear the grey and are told apart by shape and gait alone. LANTERN is the exception and it is deliberate: it is the one harmless object that costs you something, so it is allowed a colour.

The gait catalogue

A gait answers one question: what is this body doing when nothing has happened to it yet. It is a property of the type, not a roll at spawn. Twenty-two of them, each a few lines of steering, and every body in the game gets exactly one — plus any of four modifiers stacked on top.

Modifiers. face turns the body to keep its front toward the barrel, so armour can live on one side. wobble is the heading noise that already exists. planted refuses both steering-authority loss and the repeated-shove fade, so the body cannot be turned or pushed. band holds a depth band instead of closing, which is what DRIFT was given in build 298.

Every object in the game, re-gaited

Sixteen field types, and what each one should be doing instead. Eleven of the sixteen change. The twenty-seven bodies an anomaly puts on the field are out of scope: a boss choreographs its own pieces frame by frame and its minions already have distinct launches.

The twenty

Four frames each: the body at rest, the body doing the thing it does, its size against the machine and a MOTE, and its gait traced on the field. Health and speed are tier-one base values, the numbers the existing table is written in; scaleToTier multiplies them the same way it does everything else. Wave economy, weights and gates are deliberately not decided here.

Building it

Before any of it

Steering is the one thing ORDINAL's hash is most sensitive to, and the probe runs a tier-one fight with the era-one field. Take the hash on HEAD, in the same container, before the first edit — and expect a move on any phase that touches drive, because that is what these phases are. Record the delta and its cause; a phase that adds a gait nothing in that fight uses should come back identical, and a surprise there is the bug the hash exists to catch.

Left open, on purpose

  • Wave economy. No weights, no opens thresholds, no authored waves. Twenty bodies arriving on the ladder is its own measurement pass with tiers.mjs, and it cannot be done honestly until the gaits exist to measure.
  • Salvage. drops counts are sketched from mass and nothing more. The two harmless objects that can leave the field with their salvage — EMBER and LANTERN — are an economy question, not a gait question.
  • Which of the twenty ship together. Six of them (LOOM's thread, VEIL's occlusion, MIRE's ground, KITE's lob, CHAFF's ghosts, ANVIL's immunity) each add a rule the player has to be taught. They should not arrive in one build.
  • Names. Field objects are plain nouns and anomaly pieces are mathematical terms; all twenty follow the first convention. SHARD, ECHO, MITE, PLATE, SEED, CREST, BOUND and TILE are already taken by anomaly pieces and are avoided.
